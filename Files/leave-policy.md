# Leave Policy

**Company:** Acme Corp
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Applies To:** All full-time and part-time employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the types of leave available to employees, the process for requesting leave, and the responsibilities of both employees and managers in managing time away from work. Acme Corp is committed to supporting employees' health, family needs, and overall wellbeing through a fair and transparent leave system.

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Annual/Vacation Leave
- Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued monthly.
- Part-time employees accrue vacation on a pro-rated basis according to hours worked.
- A maximum of **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next calendar year; any excess is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.
- Vacation requests should be submitted at least **2 weeks in advance** through the HR portal and are subject to manager approval based on team coverage needs.

### 2.2 Sick Leave
- Employees are entitled to **10 paid sick days** per year for personal illness or injury.
- For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required.
- Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out upon separation.

### 2.3 Parental Leave
- Primary caregivers are eligible for **16 weeks** of paid parental leave.
- Secondary caregivers are eligible for **6 weeks** of paid parental leave.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- Employees should notify HR at least **30 days** before the anticipated start of leave where possible.

### 2.4 Bereavement Leave
- Employees may take up to **5 paid days** upon the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling).
- Up to **2 paid days** are available for the death of an extended family member.

### 2.5 Personal/Unpaid Leave
- Employees who have exhausted other leave types may request unpaid personal leave of up to **4 weeks**, subject to manager and HR approval.
- Extended unpaid leave beyond 4 weeks requires senior leadership sign-off and may affect benefits continuity.

### 2.6 Public Holidays
- Employees receive paid time off for all company-recognized public holidays, as published annually on the internal HR calendar.
- Employees required to work on a public holiday are entitled to a substitute day off or holiday pay, per local regulations.

## 3. Requesting Leave

1. Submit a leave request through the HR portal with the relevant dates and leave type.
2. Your manager will review and approve/deny the request within **3 business days**.
3. Approved leave is automatically reflected in the shared team calendar.
4. For sick leave taken with little or no notice, notify your manager as early as possible on the day of absence and log it in the portal upon return.

## 4. Leave During Notice Periods

Employees serving a resignation or termination notice period may not take vacation leave during that period unless otherwise agreed with HR and their manager.

## 5. Manager Responsibilities

- Ensure adequate team coverage when approving leave.
- Respond to leave requests promptly and communicate any denial with clear reasoning.
- Track direct reports' leave balances and encourage usage of accrued vacation time.

## 6. Exceptions and Local Law

Where local labor law provides more generous leave entitlements than stated in this policy, local law will take precedence. HR maintains country-specific addenda for all applicable jurisdictions.

## 7. Questions

For questions about this policy or your individual leave balance, contact **hr@acmecorp.com**.

*This policy is reviewed annually and may be updated at the company's discretion. Employees will be notified of material changes.*
