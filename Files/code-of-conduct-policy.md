# Code of Conduct Policy

**Company:** Acme Corp
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and interns

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behavior expected of everyone at Acme Corp. It is designed to foster a respectful, safe, and productive workplace, and to protect the company's reputation, assets, and relationships with customers and partners.

## 2. Core Principles

- **Respect:** Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with dignity and courtesy, regardless of role, background, or seniority.
- **Integrity:** Act honestly in all business dealings and avoid conflicts of interest.
- **Accountability:** Take ownership of your work and decisions, and raise concerns when something isn't right.
- **Compliance:** Follow applicable laws, regulations, and internal policies at all times.

## 3. Workplace Behavior

### 3.1 Respect and Anti-Harassment
- Acme Corp has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind, including based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic.
- This applies to in-person interactions, virtual meetings, internal messaging platforms, and company-sponsored events.
- Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it via the process outlined in Section 7.

### 3.2 Diversity and Inclusion
- Acme Corp is committed to building a diverse workforce and an inclusive culture where all employees can contribute and grow.
- Discriminatory remarks, jokes, or imagery — even if intended as humor — are not acceptable in any company setting.

### 3.3 Health and Safety
- Employees must comply with all workplace health and safety guidelines, whether working from an office or remotely.
- Any unsafe conditions or incidents must be reported to facilities/HR immediately.
- Use of alcohol or drugs that impairs an employee's ability to work safely is prohibited during work hours or at company events where job duties continue.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

- Employees must avoid situations where personal interests could conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of Acme Corp.
- Examples include: holding a significant financial interest in a competitor, supplier, or client; hiring or supervising a family member without disclosure; or accepting gifts that could influence business decisions.
- Any potential conflict of interest must be disclosed to your manager and HR as soon as it arises.

## 5. Confidentiality and Data Protection

- Employees must protect confidential company information, including customer data, trade secrets, and internal financial information.
- Confidential information should never be shared externally without proper authorization, and access should be limited to what is necessary for your role.
- Employees must follow the company's data security guidelines, including use of approved tools for storing and transmitting sensitive information.
- Confidentiality obligations continue after an employee's departure from the company.

## 6. Use of Company Resources

- Company equipment, systems, and accounts should be used primarily for business purposes; reasonable personal use is permitted as long as it does not interfere with work or violate other policies.
- Employees may not use company resources for illegal activity, personal financial gain outside approved arrangements, or to create or distribute offensive material.
- All company property must be returned upon separation from the company.

## 7. Reporting Concerns

- Employees are encouraged to raise concerns about violations of this Code through their manager, HR, or the confidential ethics hotline at **ethics@acmecorp.com**.
- Reports may be made anonymously where local law permits.
- Acme Corp strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation.

## 8. Investigations and Consequences

- All reported concerns will be reviewed and, where appropriate, investigated promptly and confidentially.
- Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, depending on the severity and nature of the violation.
- Where conduct may violate the law, Acme Corp reserves the right to involve external authorities.

## 9. Acknowledgment

All employees are required to review and acknowledge this Code of Conduct upon hire and annually thereafter via the HR portal.

## 10. Questions

For questions about this policy, contact **hr@acmecorp.com** or **ethics@acmecorp.com** for sensitive matters.

*This policy is reviewed annually and may be updated to reflect changes in law or company practice. Employees will be notified of material changes.*
