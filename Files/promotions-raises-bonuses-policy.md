# Promotions, Raises, and Bonuses Policy

**Company:** Acme Corp
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Applies To:** All full-time employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Acme Corp evaluates and administers promotions, salary increases, and bonus payments. The goal is to ensure decisions are consistent, merit-based, and clearly communicated across all teams.

## 2. Guiding Principles

- **Merit-based:** Advancement decisions are based on demonstrated performance, impact, and readiness for the next level of responsibility.
- **Transparency:** Employees can access leveling criteria and compensation bands for their role family through the internal Compensation Framework.
- **Consistency:** The same evaluation standards apply across teams, adjusted only for role-specific competencies.
- **Timeliness:** Compensation and promotion cycles run on a predictable annual schedule, with limited exceptions described below.

## 3. Promotions

### 3.1 Eligibility
- Employees must have been in their current role for a minimum of **12 months** before being considered for promotion, except in cases of significant scope change or business need.
- Employees must be meeting or exceeding expectations in their most recent performance review.

### 3.2 Process
1. **Nomination:** Managers nominate employees for promotion during the annual calibration cycle, or on a rolling basis for exceptional cases.
2. **Case Review:** The manager submits a promotion packet outlining the employee's impact, scope of work, and evidence of operating at the next level.
3. **Calibration:** A cross-functional panel reviews promotion cases within each department to ensure consistency across teams.
4. **Decision & Communication:** Final decisions are communicated to employees within **2 weeks** of calibration, along with feedback regardless of outcome.

### 3.3 Timing
Promotions are processed twice per year, in **April** and **October**, aligned with performance review cycles. Off-cycle promotions may occur for significant role changes but require VP-level approval.

## 4. Salary Raises

### 4.1 Annual Merit Increases
- All employees are eligible for a merit-based salary review once per year, tied to the performance review cycle.
- Increases are guided by a merit matrix that considers performance rating and position within the salary band.
- There is no guaranteed minimum increase; raises reflect individual performance and company budget for the cycle.

### 4.2 Market Adjustments
- Compensation bands are benchmarked annually against market data.
- Employees whose pay falls significantly below the updated band may receive a market adjustment independent of performance-based raises.

### 4.3 Off-Cycle Raises
Off-cycle raises may be granted in cases such as:
- A retention risk following an external offer
- A significant, sustained increase in scope without a formal promotion
- Correction of an internal pay equity issue identified during an audit

Off-cycle raises require manager, HR Business Partner, and department head approval.

## 5. Bonuses

### 5.1 Annual Performance Bonus
- Eligible employees may receive an annual bonus based on a combination of **company performance**, **team performance**, and **individual performance rating**.
- Target bonus percentages vary by role level and are outlined in each employee's offer letter or leveling documentation.
- Bonuses are paid out in **Q1** following the close of the fiscal year, contingent on the employee being employed on the payout date.

### 5.2 Spot Bonuses
- Managers may award discretionary spot bonuses for exceptional, immediate contributions (e.g., successfully leading a critical project).
- Spot bonuses require HR approval and are capped per manager per quarter.

### 5.3 Signing and Referral Bonuses
- Signing bonuses may be offered as part of a new hire's offer package, typically subject to a repayment clause if the employee departs within 12 months.
- Referral bonuses are paid to employees who refer a candidate who is hired and completes a **90-day** probation period, per the referral program terms.

## 6. Performance Ratings and Compensation Linkage

Compensation decisions are directly informed by the annual performance review rating scale (Exceeds, Meets, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory). Employees rated "Needs Improvement" or below are not eligible for merit increases or bonuses in that cycle, though they remain eligible for market adjustments.

## 7. Confidentiality

Individual compensation details are confidential between the employee, their manager, and HR. Employees are, however, free to discuss their own pay openly if they choose, consistent with applicable pay transparency laws.

## 8. Questions

For questions about your compensation, band, or promotion readiness, speak with your manager or contact **compensation@acmecorp.com**.

*This policy is reviewed annually by HR and Finance and may be adjusted based on business performance and market conditions.*
